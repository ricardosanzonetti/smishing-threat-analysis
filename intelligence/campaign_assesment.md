# Smishing Campaign Assessment

## Executive Summary

This assessment evaluates a simulated SMS-based social engineering campaign impersonating ING banking services through voice-call redirection techniques.

The campaign characteristics are consistent with financially motivated smishing operations commonly targeting mobile banking users through urgency-based psychological manipulation.

The investigation identified multiple indicators associated with credential theft pretexting and fraudulent customer support impersonation workflows.

---

## Campaign Characteristics

| Category | Observation |
|---|---|
| Threat Type | SMS Phishing (Smishing) |
| Impersonated Entity | ING Banking Services |
| Delivery Method | SMS Social Engineering |
| Primary Objective | Credential Theft |
| Secondary Objective | Voice-call Redirection |
| Victim Targeting | Spanish Banking Users |
| Confidence Level | High |

---

## Behavioral Analysis

### Psychological Manipulation Techniques

- Urgency-based coercion
- Fear-inducing account compromise alerts
- Banking authority impersonation
- Social trust exploitation
- Voice support escalation pressure

---

### Operational Characteristics

- Mobile-oriented delivery
- Passive credential harvesting pretext
- Fraudulent support interaction attempt
- Low-complexity social engineering workflow
- Financially motivated targeting behavior

---

## Threat Intelligence Assessment

The observed campaign behavior aligns with low-to-medium complexity financially motivated phishing operations frequently observed in mobile banking fraud ecosystems.

No malware delivery indicators or exploit deployment artifacts were identified during the investigation process.

The operational methodology instead focused primarily on psychological manipulation and credential acquisition attempts.
